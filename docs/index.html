Schrödinger’s Charge

What happens when a ₹499 payment goes through, but the merchant times out?

On Pay, the provider saves ₹499 and delays its reply by 2 seconds. The merchant times out after 500 ms and stays pending.

Diagram and trace reference

Arrows show requests and replies. A dashed arrow marks a failed request. The merchant and provider each store their own state in SQLite.

GET checks for a charge. POST sends a charge request. 201 means a new charge. 200 returns an existing charge. 404 means missing. 503 means unavailable.

Purple branches group the steps. Green dots mark a confirmation. Rose dots mark a failure.

The payment ID is the idempotency key. The provider’s SQLite database allows one charge per ID, even when two workers retry.
